Every change the `hallow` branch of [xena-studios/calagopus-panel](https://github.com/xena-studios/calagopus-panel) makes outside the Hallow extension. [Panel](../docs/PANEL.md) allows only two kinds: branding, and hooks an extension needs. Each one is offered upstream; the drafts are in [upstream-drafts/](upstream-drafts/).

Upstream base: `calagopus/panel` `main` at `f64ff1b0e` (v1.2.10), forked on 2026-11-15.

## Generated by the extension tooling, not hand edits

`panel-rs extensions init dev.xenastudios.hallow` and `panel-rs extensions resync` write these. Upstream's tree has them empty because it ships no extensions; they change whenever an extension is added or removed, and a merge conflict in them is fixed by re-running `panel-rs extensions resync`.

- `backend-extensions/internal-list/Cargo.toml` and `src/lib.rs`: the list of compiled-in extensions.
- `Cargo.lock` and `frontend/pnpm-lock.yaml`: the new workspace members.
- Symlinks `frontend/extensions/dev_xenastudios_hallow`, `database/extension-migrations/dev_xenastudios_hallow` and `backend-extensions/node_modules`. A fresh clone needs the last one: without it, the extension's frontend can't resolve the panel's packages and `pnpm build:ci` fails.

## Deliberately unchanged

- The footer keeps a Calagopus credit ("Hallow Panel © 2026 Xena Studios · built on Calagopus"): the MIT licence asks us to keep the notice. The footer and sidebar header are replaced through the core's build-time component override hook from the extension, not by editing core files.
- The OOBE and other user-facing "Calagopus" strings are patched at runtime by the extension in English only; the other 18 languages still say Calagopus. The setup wizard's steps are replaced by the extension (Welcome, Account, Panel settings, Control plane, Finish); the control-plane step reuses the stored `repositories` step key because the backend only accepts its fixed list. The `alt` text in `AppIcon.tsx` is unchanged.
- The `Calagopus-User` request header, the CLI's copyright line and the systemd unit description: not shown to panel users.
